FAQ

Questions, answered

The things clients usually ask before we start.

What platforms do you build on?

GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels and WordPress, automated with Zapier and n8n and connected to Stripe and webhooks, so ads, leads, AI agents, payments and follow-up all flow through one system.

Can you build AI agents for my business?

Yes. I build Voice AI agents that handle calls and Conversation AI agents that reply to chats and messages. They're connected to your CRM, so they can qualify leads and book appointments.

Can you set up or fix my GoHighLevel account?

Yes. I do full account setups (pipelines, workflows, tags and A2P registration) and also untangle and extend existing accounts.

Do you also run the ads?

Yes. I run Meta and Google Ads connected directly to the funnel and CRM, so every new lead is tagged and followed up automatically.

Who have you worked with?

Agencies, startups and multi-brand businesses in the US and the Netherlands, across healthcare, coaching, home services, finance, beauty and SaaS.

How do we get started?

Send me an email or WhatsApp with what you're selling and where leads get stuck. I'll reply with how I'd build it.
